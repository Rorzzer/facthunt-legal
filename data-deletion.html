Delete your data

Email privacy@facthunt.app with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us one or more of:

  • your Instagram username, if you commented on or messaged @facthunt.app;
  • the link to any saved Facthunt result or shared game;
  • the email address you used to contact us.

We'll confirm by email when it's done, within 30 days.

Do it yourself

  • Game data on your device: clear your browser's site data for facthunt.app.
  • Instagram comments: delete the comment on Instagram. Our duplicate-message records hold no username or comment text and are deleted after 30 days.
  • Instagram messages: delete the conversation in Instagram. We'll also delete our copy on request.

You don't log in to Facthunt with Facebook or Instagram, so there's no app connection for you to remove. See the privacy policy for everything we handle.
