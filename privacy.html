Privacy policy

Facthunt is a trivia game at facthunt.app with an Instagram account, @facthunt.app. This page explains what information we handle and why. Questions: privacy@facthunt.app.

When you play the game

You don't need an account to play. To run your games we store game progress, answers and scores, and any display name you choose to type in. Some of this is kept in your own browser's local storage. Shared games and results you choose to save are stored in our database. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site.

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we don't sell your information.

When you interact with us on Instagram

If you comment on an @facthunt.app post, Instagram (Meta) makes that comment available to our account's tools through the official Instagram API. On posts that invite it, such as the Weekly Stumper, commenting the hint keyword may trigger one private message from us containing the hint.

To avoid sending duplicate messages, we record the comment's ID, the post it was on, the time and whether the message was delivered. We don't store your username or comment text in that record, and delivered records are deleted after 30 days. Instagram's own handling of your data is covered by Instagram's privacy policy.

When you email us

We use your email and message only to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run Facthunt: our website host, our database provider and Meta/Instagram. Some of them store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We don't share or sell your information for advertising.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data for facthunt.app to remove locally stored game data.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information: see Delete your data.
  • Delete a comment on Instagram at any time; we can't read deleted comments.

If you're unhappy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Children

Facthunt is a general-audience game. Instagram requires users to be at least 13.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date below.
